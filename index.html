( 05 ) — Questions

Straight answers. Straight answers.

What clients ask before they ship with us — answered plainly.

01

What does Ward Logistics do?

Ward Logistics Services is a specialised logistics company for complex cargo across the GCC. We move oversized machinery and project cargo, handle customs clearance in-house, coordinate door-to-door through carrier partners in over 120 countries, and transport oil and gas for public and private businesses.

02

Where is Ward Logistics based?

Our head office is in Sohar, Al Batinah, Sultanate of Oman (Postal Code 311), registered under C.R. No. 1635804. We serve clients across the GCC.

03

Can Ward Logistics ship oversized or heavy cargo?

Yes. Oversized and heavy cargo is our specialty. We engineer a tailored plan for each load — project cargo planning, route survey and feasibility, and specialized rigging — for unusual dimensions, heavy loads and hazardous materials.

04

Do you handle customs clearance?

Yes. Our own in-house customs brokers prepare all documentation, keep shipments compliant with GCC regulations and minimise delays at the border, including duty and tax optimization and compliance consulting.

05

Are there hidden fees?

No. Ward Logistics quotes an all-inclusive flat rate, with no base-rate-plus-surcharge pricing and no last-minute charges.

06

Can I track my shipment?

Yes. Shipments are monitored in real time with GPS updates rather than delayed milestone reports, and high-value assets can be monitored with IoT sensors.

07

How quickly are damage claims resolved?

We operate a zero-damage philosophy, and if a claim does arise we resolve it on an expedited 48-hour basis rather than weeks of processing.

08

Do you transport oil and gas?

Yes. Our petroleum services cover the transportation of oil and gas for public and private businesses, including tanker and transport operations as well as maintenance, operation and lodging.
